COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

What is StudentHub? +

StudentHub is a digital student portal designed to help students manage their academic activities, discover events, connect with others and access useful learning resources.

How can I create a StudentHub account? +

Click on the Register button in the navigation menu. Fill in your personal and academic information, create a password and submit the registration form.

How can I access my student dashboard? +

After logging in to StudentHub, you can access the Dashboard from the navigation menu. It provides information about courses, tasks, events and activities.

Can I register for college events? +

Yes. Visit the Events page to explore upcoming academic, technology, sports and cultural events. Select an event and use the Register Now option.

Can I update my profile information? +

Yes. Open your Profile page to view your personal and academic information. The Edit Profile option can be used to update your details.

What should I do if I forget my password? +

Use the Forgot Password option on the Login page. If you still need assistance, contact the StudentHub support team.

How can I contact StudentHub support? +

You can visit the Contact page and send us a message using the contact form. You can also find the available support contact information there.

Can students give feedback? +

Yes. Students can use the Feedback page to share their experience, suggestions and ideas for improving StudentHub.
